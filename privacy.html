1. Information We Collect

Minimal identity profile and your routine preferences.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Streako mobile application (Android package: com.ethicsofcoding.streako) developed by Ethics of Coding. When you choose to sign in using Google Authentication, we receive basic profile details including your name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google via Firebase Authentication. We also record the habit titles, target frequencies, reminder intervals, occurrence timestamps, and streak calculations you configure within the application.

2. Local-First Storage Architecture

Hive sandbox storage strictly on your local hardware.

Streako prioritizes user privacy through a local-first design. Your habit data, tracking entries, and customized reminder times are stored primarily on your device in local sandboxed storage (Hive). We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal tracking records with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

3. Notification & Alarm Permissions

Exact alarm channels strictly for scheduled rituals.

We request operating system permissions to post notifications and schedule exact alarms (iOS AlarmKit and Android Exact Alarm channels). These permissions are utilized strictly to trigger scheduled reminder alarms and notifications for your configured habits, cutting through system silencers when enabled by you.

4. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase Authentication for safe identity sync.

We utilize Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to provide secure cloud identity and seamless device switching. These third-party services process authentication tokens according to Google's rigorous Privacy Policy and industry security benchmarks.

5. Complete User Data Deletion

1-tap automated wipe in Settings erases all traces permanently.

You maintain absolute sovereignty over your data. Because Streako operates on a local-first storage model and uses only Google Single Sign-On (SSO) login without storing passwords, clicking "Delete Account" in the mobile app Settings automatically deletes your account details, permanently erases all local Hive database files, unregisters all scheduled alarm notifications, and purges your SSO profile. Read our dedicated Delete Account Guide for step-by-step instructions.

6. Data Security

TLS 1.3 encryption and device sandbox isolation.

We employ modern transport layer security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) for all network communication and sandbox-level isolation on your device to safeguard your routines against unauthorized access, corruption, or disclosure.

7. Children's Privacy

Streako is built for general audiences aged 13 and older.

Streako is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us immediately for swift removal.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Transparent revisions with updated timestamp.

We may periodically revise our Privacy Policy to reflect evolving features or legal standards. Revisions will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of Streako denotes consent to the updated terms.

9. Contact Us

Direct human support for questions or data requests.

If you have questions, feedback, or data requests regarding our Privacy Policy or privacy rights, please contact our team directly at streakocloud@gmail.com. We respond to all verified inquiries promptly.
