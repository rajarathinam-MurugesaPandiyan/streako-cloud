1. Steps to Delete Your Account Inside the App (Recommended)

If you currently have Streako installed on your mobile phone, you can immediately and permanently delete your entire account and all associated habit tracking data directly from the app:

  1. 1
    Open Streako App

    Launch the Streako mobile app on your Android or iOS device.

  2. 2
    Open Settings

    Tap the Settings icon (gear icon) located in the upper navigation bar.

  3. 3
    Locate Danger Zone

    Scroll down to the bottom of the Settings page to the Danger Zone section.

  4. 4
    Tap "Delete Account & Data"

    Tap the red Delete Account & Data button.

  5. 5
    Confirm Permanent Erasure

    Confirm the deletion prompt. Your local database (Hive) is wiped immediately, scheduled notifications and alarms are cancelled, and your Google Single Sign-On (SSO) session is permanently terminated.

2. Steps to Request Account Deletion Without the App (Web / Email Request)

If you have uninstalled the Streako app, lost access to your device, or signed in using Google Authentication and wish to request complete deletion of your account and credentials, follow these steps:

  1. 1
    Send Email from Registered Address

    Send an email from the Google email address associated with your Streako account to streakocloud@gmail.com.

  2. 2
    Use Required Subject Line

    Set subject to: Streako Account & Data Deletion Request (com.ethicsofcoding.streako).

  3. 3
    Provide Identification Details

    Confirm your registered Google email address and state that you request permanent deletion of your profile and all associated data.

  4. 4
    Verification & Confirmation

    Our security team will authenticate your request, delete your Firebase authentication profile, and send an email confirmation within 48 to 72 hours.

Launch Pre-Filled Deletion Email (streakocloud@gmail.com)

3. Types of Data Deleted vs. Kept & Retention Period

Streako adheres strictly to the Google Play Store Data Safety and Account Deletion policy:

Types of Data Permanently Deleted:

  • User Identity & Profile: Google Single Sign-On UID, email address, name, and profile photo URL stored in Firebase Authentication.
  • Habit Definitions & Targets: All custom habit titles, descriptions, schedules, frequency rules, and reminder intervals.
  • Streak & Completion Records: Historical completion timestamps, check-in logs, current streaks, best streak records, and unlocked awards.
  • System Alarms & Notifications: All scheduled AlarmKit alarms and notification channels are immediately deregistered and cancelled.
  • Local Device Cache: Local preferences, audio soundscape selections, and theme configuration.

Types of Data Kept:

NONE (Personal & Habit Data). Streako retains zero personal profile data, habit records, or authentication tokens once account deletion is confirmed. Underlying Firebase / Google Cloud edge infrastructure retains only anonymous, ephemeral HTTP access/error logs for DDoS protection and abuse prevention, which rotate automatically within 30 days.

Additional Retention Period:

Zero Days for User Data (up to 30 days for ephemeral HTTP edge logs). Deletion performed within the app takes effect immediately on your account and hardware credentials. Manual deletion requests sent by email are processed within 48 to 72 business hours, with no secondary marketing databases or delayed personal archive queues.
